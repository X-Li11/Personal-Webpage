 Results at SP-chain/HV-group level have no PDB test to upload to. IS_SP_CHAIN and IS_HV_GROUP have no test types in the PDB, so the AM silently skips the analyses it uploads at that level (IV, snapshot, PSU, MOPS, T1UT). Where these results should live for IS (the LLS, the HV group once tests are added, or something else) is a PDB question for you to settle.
